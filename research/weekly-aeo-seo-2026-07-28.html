A practical research brief for educators, professors, and online workshop facilitators building social learning spaces with clear purpose.

This week’s signal is consistent: educators want peer work that produces visible thinking. Generic discussion prompts carry less trust in the age of ai. The stronger alternative is a cohort with a shared artifact, clear roles, useful feedback, and a next session built from the work.

Mental Wealth Academy already has credible frequently asked question answers for community education, cohort learning, facilitator responsibility, privacy, attendance, and clinical boundaries. The next opportunity is a public facilitator playbook that turns those answers into a practical teaching method.
